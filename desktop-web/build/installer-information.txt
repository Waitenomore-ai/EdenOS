EDEN'S GARDEN

Welcome to Eden's Garden, a calm, child-friendly space to learn, play, watch and grow.

The desktop application securely connects to:
https://www.edensgarden.uk/app/unified/

An internet connection is required for the online learning activities. Some Story Garden content may be available offline after it has been downloaded or cached in the app.

Support: hello@edensgarden.uk
Website: https://edensgarden.uk

IMPORTANT
This installer installs the Eden's Garden desktop application. It does not install or replace the full Eden OS operating system.

Eden's Garden is provided for families to use in accordance with the current website terms and privacy information.
